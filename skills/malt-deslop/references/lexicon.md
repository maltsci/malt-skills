# Chinese AI-writing lexicon

Words are not evidence by themselves. Use this reference after repairing the passage’s reasoning and structure. Change a word when it clusters with other signals or stands in for a concrete fact; otherwise leave appropriate vocabulary alone.

## Marketing-grade intensifiers

Examples: `拉满`、`硬核`、`封神`、`天花板`、`顶级`、`终极`、`炸裂`、`王炸`、`全网最`、`不愧是`.

Name the degree instead: “twice as fast as last week,” “reduced three manual steps,” or an appropriately qualified comparison.

## Empty abstraction and management jargon

Examples: `底层逻辑`、`底层原理`、`底层方法`、`底层诉求`、`文化密码`、`注脚`、`颗粒度`、`抓手`、`闭环`、`赋能`、`打法`、`对齐`、`重构`.

Replace the abstraction with the relevant mechanism: incentives, rules, interfaces, a workflow, a decision, a constraint, or an observed result. Preserve a term when it is established technical vocabulary in the discipline.

## Presenter promises

Examples: `掰开揉碎`、`一次性讲透`、`拆分清楚`、`说白了`、`不绕弯子`、`最通俗最简单`、`先说结论`、`重点来了`、`让我解释一下`.

Start with the content. If the conclusion belongs first, put it there without announcing it.

## Emotional staging

Examples: `后背发凉`、`脊背发凉`、`头皮发麻`、`让所有人沉默`、`揪心`、`毛骨悚然`、`更扎心`、`狠狠打脸`.

Replace the staged feeling with the underlying consequence, loss, risk, or decision.

## Overforce and packaging

Examples: `凿`、`填`、`甩`、`钉`、`砸`、`撕开`、`死死`、`锚定`; headline-like standalone evaluations such as `稳`、`静`、`冷`.

Prefer a plain verb unless force is literal and specific.

## Absolutes and self-importance

Examples: `真正的`、`实际上是`、`恰恰是`、`完美符合`、`极致`、`所有人`、`从来`、`永远`、`这才是关键`、`大家都忽略了`.

Narrow the subject and give the evidence: who, when, compared with what, and under what condition.

## Formatting habits

- Dense em dashes (`——`) used as dramatic pauses or false-contrast scaffolding.
- Quotation marks around ordinary words to manufacture a concept.
- A colon after every sentence as a default list structure.
- Choppy slogan fragments: `快速。简单。` or `不废话。只给答案。`
- Numbered, presenter-style headings followed by `今天我们就……`.

These forms may be correct in isolation. Repair them only when they make the text repetitive or performative.

## Common formulae

The following are usually better rewritten from their intended meaning:

- `真正的 X 从来不是 Y，而是 Z`
- `这不是 X，这是 Y`
- `不是锤子，更像手术刀`
- `它是某某里的 Excel`
- `先说结论` + `不玩半点虚的`
- `更扎心的是` + `后背发凉`
- `更快、更便宜、更聪明`
- `你以为这就完了`
- `总之，以上就是……`
- `通往……的旅程，始于……`

## Adjacent generic phrases

Examples: `值得注意的是`、`综上所述`、`在当今社会`、`随着……的不断发展`、`为我们提供了宝贵的启示`、`不可否认`、`从某种意义上说`、`落地`、`打磨`、`沉淀`.

Delete the throat-clearing, then see whether a specific claim remains. If not, develop the claim rather than replacing it with another general phrase.
