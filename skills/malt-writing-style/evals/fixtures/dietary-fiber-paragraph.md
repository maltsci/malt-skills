# Scientific-methods rewrite fixture

## Source paragraph

膳食纤维变化可能同时影响菌群与短链脂肪酸。如果干预伴随其他饮食改变，不能仅凭结果变化把作用归给盐；需在设计、测量和分析计划中处理竞争性解释。也就是说，混杂风险不是在结果出来后用文字补救，而应在形成草稿前标为未决事项，并明确由原始来源、指南或研究团队决定。

## Invariants

- Dietary-fiber changes may affect both gut microbiota and short-chain fatty acids.
- When an intervention also changes other dietary factors, observed changes alone cannot be attributed to salt.
- Competing explanations belong in the design, measurement, and analysis plan.
- Confounding must be marked as unresolved before drafting and settled by primary sources, guidelines, or the research team.
- Do not add war imagery, places, people, or any claim of causal proof.
